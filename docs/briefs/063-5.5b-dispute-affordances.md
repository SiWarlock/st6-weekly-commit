## Feature
On the plan read (E3 `GET /api/plans/current`, E4 `GET /api/plans/{id}`), compute the dispute `allowedActions` for the **viewing actor**: a direct manager viewing a report's plan sees `OPEN_DISPUTE` (per eligible commitment) + `RESOLVE_DISPUTE` (per unresolved nested dispute); the owning IC sees `RESPOND_DISPUTE` (per OPEN nested dispute). Establishes the **`viewerIsDirectManager` threading** through `PlanMapper` (reused later by Phase-6 MARK_REVIEWED).
