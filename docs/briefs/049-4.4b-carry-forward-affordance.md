## Feature

## Things to flag at Step 2.5
1. **[THE PREDICATE] Which commitments get `CARRY_FORWARD`?** The E12 endpoint accepts carry-forward on ANY commitment in an owned RECONCILING plan (it overwrites the outcome; re-carry is idempotent). The affordance may match that exactly OR narrow it for UX. My default: **owner ∧ plan RECONCILING ∧ own-row ∧ `reconciliationOutcome != CARRIED_FORWARD`** (hide once already carried — re-carry is a pointless idempotent no-op, so don't surface it; the endpoint still protects against a double-submit). **Sub-question:** also restrict to *incomplete* commitments (no completion outcome), i.e. only surface "carry forward" on not-yet-done work? My vote: **no** — let the IC carry forward anything not-already-carried (a `PARTIALLY_COMPLETED`/`BLOCKED` item is the prime carry-forward case; even a `COMPLETED`-but-continuing item is the IC's call). Confirm the predicate — note this fully determines the UX (the frontend defers entirely to `allowedActions`).
2. **Which response paths thread the context?** The nested plan read (E3/E4 via `PlanMapper`) is the must-have (that's what the IC's reconciliation view consumes). The single-commitment write responses (E11 create, E12 carry-forward response, E6 PATCH) — thread plan+actor too for consistency, or leave empty (the frontend re-reads the plan)? My default: **thread it on the plan read (must-have) + the E12 response** (so the just-carried successor/source reflect correctly); E5/E6/E7/E11 single responses can stay empty (the UI re-reads). Confirm — or thread everywhere for uniformity if cheap.
3. **`commitmentActions` shape.** A new `AllowedActionResolver.commitmentActions(actorEmployeeId, plan, commitment)` returning `List<AllowedAction>` (extensible — OPEN_DISPUTE/COMMENT join later by their enforcing slices). Confirm the resolver method (vs inlining in the mapper) — the resolver keeps affordance↔enforcement single-source.
